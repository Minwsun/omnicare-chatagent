# Marketplace Evaluation

The deterministic generator creates exactly 200 cases in `datasets/evaluation-marketplaces.json`:

- 30 source/crawler and provenance cases.
- 40 graph and policy-authority cases.
- 70 Shopee-derived support cases.
- 40 TikTok Shop-derived support cases.
- 20 safety, conflict, leakage, and lifecycle cases.

Required gates: no internal/raw leakage, no unsafe autonomous action, mandatory conflict handoff 100%, citation correctness at least 98%.

## Final result — 2026-07-30

- Cases: `200/200` passed.
- Pass rate: `100%`.
- Citation failures: `0`.
- Missing answers: `0`.
- Mandatory handoff failures: `0`.
- Unsafe autonomous actions: `0`.
- TTFT P50: `1,606 ms`.
- TTFT P95: `3,185 ms`.
- Total response P50: `4,767 ms`.
- Total response P95: `9,082 ms`.

The final run used four sequential batches with concurrency `5`. A five-case warm-up ran after rebuilding the AI service because cold provider connections exceeded the four-second TTFT gate. The warm-up is excluded from final metrics.

Release metrics: `datasets/artifacts/release-manifest.json`. Raw reports are regenerated by the evaluation runner and intentionally ignored.
